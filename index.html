📘 Simple Farm Guide

1. Choose a plant

Choose a crop suitable for your climate, space, and season.

2. Prepare the soil

Use suitable soil with good drainage and remove large stones or weeds.

3. Plant carefully

Follow the seed packet for planting depth and spacing.

4. Water correctly

Check soil moisture and avoid leaving roots in standing water.

5. Give enough light

Check each plant's sunlight needs; some crops prefer cooler or partly shaded conditions.

6. Observe and harvest

Watch for changes and harvest when the crop reaches its usual maturity.
